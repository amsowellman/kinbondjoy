Poopy and Friends

The comic-relief hero with a heart of gold and a smell of pure chaos.

Ep. 1 — The Fun-Freezer 3000

Dr. Robotnik freezes ALL the fun in town. Sonic bonks, Donkey Kong barrels, and one heroic heap of brown saves the day with the Silly Dance of the Seven Stinkwaves — featuring two brand-new songs, "The Poopy Theme" and "Giggle Storm."

7 minutes · full voice cast · two songs · silliest episode yet

Ep. 2 — The Perpetual Monday Machine

A machine that makes every day Monday. Forever. Poopy and the gang fight back with the one weapon Mondays cannot survive: Tuesday energy.

7 minutes · full voice cast · Monday vs. Tuesday

Ep. 3 — The Fun Permit Office

Doctor Robotnik tries to permit away all fun with Form 27-B — and drowns in his own self-filing paperwork.

8 minutes · full voice cast · strictly no fun ( Form 27-B )

Ep. 4 — The Robotnik Repeaters

Eight clones of Doctor Robotnik, one maximum-goof duplication machine, a blanket-fort pizza party, and a ninth Robotnik loose somewhere in town.

9 minutes · nine Robotniks · conga line

Ep. 5 — The Duck Situation

Robotnik number nine — the one competent copy — trains forty ducks for Operation Featherstorm, then learns that evil things do not wait for baby ducks stuck in drains. Ten-for-ten giggle confirmed.

11 minutes · forty ducks · one parade

Ep. 6 — The Great Sneak Retirement

Robotnik number nine has been doing secret good deeds for four days — and a new friend, Momo the museum monkey who sees EVERYTHING, teaches him that sneaking is better with a friend. Plus: three baby ducks, one catastrophic naming error, and a first word nobody was ready for.

13 minutes · Momo the monkey · sneak scarf retired
